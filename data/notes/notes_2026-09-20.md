## UA-2026-09-03-014431-a
Чернігів, Аварійно-рятувальний загін спецпризначення ДСНС — 14 × vehicle-mounted short-range EW jammers (ЗРЕБ), budget 7,973,000 (569,500/unit). Spec «Додаток 2»: four bands with gap-free coverage required — 200–1700, 1950–2950, 3200–3450 and 5700–6000 MHz, all omnidirectional 360°; the acceptance test is suppression of an FPV control link at ≥70 m with the operator 3 km away (1 W remote, 9 dBi antenna, approach from 50 m descending to 10 m, repeated with the unit turned 90°). Closed aerodynamic composite housing, roof mount by bolts or magnets, IP54, radio-transparent finish in a colour the buyer picks. Kit: remote operator console on ≥3 m cable with band switching and fault indication; separate LiFePO4 power pack for ≥90 min of emission, ≤120 min charge from 220 V, IP54, full protection set. 12-month warranty on each part. Delivery to Чернігів by 30 Nov 2026. Status: in qualification with **one bid, 7,840,000** (560,000/unit, −1.7 % vs budget) — no auction, no competition. The 200–1700 MHz block starting at 200 MHz and the 5.7–6 GHz block are the unusual parts; most municipal РЕБ specs stop at 300–1020 + 2.1–2.7 GHz.
